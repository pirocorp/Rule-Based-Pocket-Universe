# S01E01 — Evidence Notes

- Monsters appear human, speak, manipulate, and do not simply brute-force every encounter.
- Talismans are used as protection.

- Driving away loops back to the town.
- Crows/ravens appear around the fallen tree.
- Sarah is introduced as an apparently ordinary resident.
