# S01E08 — Evidence Notes

## Boyd background / town evolution
- The episode shows Boyd's arrival and how the settlement's survival model evolved.
- Before talismans, people relied on hiding.
- Boyd discovers talismans and changes the community's survival strategy.

## Talismans
- Talismans bear rune-like markings.
- This raises the possibility of structured writing/code/symbolic semantics.

## Voices / Father Khatri question
A major unresolved logical question:

> If Sarah's voices had knowledge of Father Khatri burying his belongings, why do they not apparently use such observation to tell the monsters where people hide?

This strengthens the need to avoid assuming voices = monsters.

## Abby
- Boyd's wife is Abby.
- Her breakdown and violence are a major part of the flashback.
- Running joke from the discussion: she evokes "Sarah Connor" vibes, except the real Sarah Connor would probably try to exterminate the monsters.

## Rule impact

- ADR-005 symbols-as-language becomes worth tracking.
