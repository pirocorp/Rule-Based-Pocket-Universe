# S01E04 — Evidence Notes

## Victor / Boy in White
- Young Victor is shown after a catastrophic event in the town.
- The Boy in White appears in connection with young Victor.
- Adult Victor tells Ethan that the Boy in White previously chose him and now appears to have chosen Ethan.
- Victor warns Ethan that others may not understand/believe discussion of the Boy in White.
- Ethan asks about Victor's parents; Victor avoids the question.

## Faraway Tree
- Victor demonstrates a Faraway Tree by moving an object through it.
- This is direct evidence that ordinary spatial adjacency does not apply everywhere.

## Sarah / Ethan
- Sarah's anomalous message directs her to kill "the boy" (Ethan), making Ethan's importance a major question.

## Hypothesis impact
- Pocket-reality model strengthened.
- Multiple-forces hypothesis becomes plausible: voices target Ethan while Boy in White contacts him.
