# Current State

**Knowledge cutoff:** S01E08  
**Model version:** 0.1

## Primary working model

### Rule-Driven Pocket Universe
**Confidence:** 4.5 / 5

The town appears to be a closed or topologically unusual environment accessible from geographically unrelated real-world locations. It follows consistent but unfamiliar rules. The term "pocket universe" is also voiced as an in-story possibility by a minor character in S01E07, which makes it an explicitly contemplated hypothesis inside the narrative, not a confirmation.

## Strongest established rules

- **UR-001 Entry Rule:** arrival is associated with a fallen tree.
- **UR-002 Topological Closure Rule:** attempts to leave by road return to the town.
- **UR-003 Night Cycle Rule:** monsters operate at night; none have been shown active in daylight.
- **UR-004 Anomalous Infrastructure Rule:** electricity functions although the wiring/infrastructure does not make normal physical sense.
- **UR-005 Rule Discoverability:** humans can discover and exploit previously unknown rules, most clearly with the talismans.
- **ER-001 Monster Behaviour:** monsters are intelligent, calm, manipulative, and patterned rather than chaotic.
- **ER-002 Protected-Enclosure Rule:** talismans protect enclosed spaces under conditions not fully understood.
- **ER-003 Faraway Tree Rule:** Faraway Trees can relocate objects/people.
- **KR-001 Selective Information Rule:** different people receive different anomalous information channels.
- **KR-002 Recurring Symbol Rule:** Jade's symbol predates his arrival and appears in an older book; talismans also carry rune-like markings, though a connection is not yet established.
- **KR-003 Victor Legacy Knowledge:** Victor has long-term experiential knowledge but avoids or struggles with parts of his past.

## Active hypotheses

1. **Rule-Driven Pocket Universe** — strongest.
2. **Multiple forces / non-unified agency** — plausible; voices and Boy in White may not share goals.
3. **The environment exposes information selectively** — strongly supported.
4. **Symbols may form a language/code** — emerging, not proven.
5. **The world's apparent human infrastructure may imitate outcomes without normal implementation** — plausible after the electricity investigation.

## Most important open questions

- Are the voices, monsters, and Boy in White part of one agency or multiple agencies?

- What are the exact constraints of talismans?
- What determines Faraway Tree destinations?
- Why Victor, Ethan, Sarah, and Jade?
